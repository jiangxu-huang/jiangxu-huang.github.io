huangjx1024@126.com Google Scholar ResearchGate GitHub

I am a Ph.D. student at the Center for Complex System Simulation, School of Mathematics and Statistics, Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My work uses mesoscopic numerical methods to understand complex fluid flows and interfacial phenomena.我是华中科技大学数学与统计学院复杂系统仿真研究中心的博士研究生，主要采用介观数值方法研究复杂流体流动与界面现象。

My research focuses on gas–liquid–solid multiphase flows and phase-change phenomena using diffuse-interface models and lattice Boltzmann methods. My interests include droplet and bubble dynamics, particularly droplet impact; active-particle motion (squirmers and phoretic particles); surfactant-mediated interfacial flows; and phase transitions, including boiling, condensation, evaporation, and droplet freezing.我的研究聚焦气–液–固多相流与相变现象，主要采用弥散界面模型和格子 Boltzmann 方法。研究兴趣包括液滴与气泡动力学（尤其是液滴撞击）、活性颗粒运动（squirmers 和 phoretic particles）、表面活性剂调控的界面流动，以及沸腾、凝结、蒸发和液滴冻结等相变过程。

Research研究方向

Multiphase & interfacial flows多相流与界面动力学

Phase-field descriptions of evolving interfaces, wetting, and surfactant transport in complex fluid systems.采用相场方法描述复杂流体中的界面演化、润湿和表面活性剂输运。

Solid–liquid phase change固液相变与传热

Coupled interface dynamics and heat transfer during melting and solidification in multiphase systems.研究多相体系熔化与凝固过程中的界面演化及其与传热的耦合。

Active particles & microswimming活性颗粒与微游动

Numerical modeling of self-propelled particles and their hydrodynamic interactions at small scales.开展自驱动颗粒及其微尺度流体动力学相互作用的数值建模。

Methods: phase-field modeling, lattice Boltzmann methods, and mesoscopic numerical simulation.研究方法：相场建模、格子 Boltzmann 方法与介观数值模拟。
